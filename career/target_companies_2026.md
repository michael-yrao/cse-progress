# Target Companies — Senior, Weak-SD-Friendly (2026)

**Filters applied:** aiming senior · system design is the weak axis right now (target loops where design
is a practical conversation or take-home, not a rubric-scored SD gauntlet) · gaming industry excluded ·
under-the-radar welcome.

## Two caveats that gate everything

1. **DSA fundamentals are strong, but DP and backtracking aren't started yet** (Backtracking ~Sep 14, 1D DP
   ~Oct 12, 2D DP ~Nov 9). DP/backtracking is the usual pass/fail separator at the algorithm-heavy shops.
2. **System design is unproven, not weak** — the mock board is all cold. At senior, no loop skips design;
   these clusters just weight it lightest. A handful of HelloInterview mocks is the single highest-leverage
   move — it lifts every cluster at once.
