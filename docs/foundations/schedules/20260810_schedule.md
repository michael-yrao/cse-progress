> *"I want to always have new or probe problems on days that have no system design. problems that I have
> seen more than twice will always be a bit more lenient on me."*

recognition probe, interchangeable. At `three_weekly` that is **4 unseen problems a week**. SD days carry
none; the lane at 3.0 units is already doing the hard work.

tests recognition and transfer.

*then* 🟢 backlog. ⚠️ **Intake caps still bind** (moderate 5 / hard 4 / DP 3 per week); **the probe is the
release valve**, since it creates no tracker row on 🟢 and therefore never fights the cap. Full rule:
`.claude/memory/feedback_unseen_on_non_sd_days.md`.
