## 🔤 Concept primers

**Backtracking — opens Sep 14, so the primer is scheduled Sun Sep 13** (before 22 Generate Parentheses).

- **Object + name:** *backtracking* — build a partial solution one choice at a time; when a branch is
  exhausted or invalid, **undo the last choice and try the next** (the "back" in backtracking).
- **Nearest neighbour + the one feature:** plain **DFS**, which walks the tree but **never undoes** — it has
  no notion of retracting a choice to explore a sibling. The `undo` on the way back up is the whole difference.
- **Why the obvious approach is not enough:** enumerate every full candidate then filter is exponential in
  the *wrong* exponent — backtracking prunes invalid partials early, cutting the tree before it fans out.
- **Stops there.** The procedure (choose → recurse → undo) is the first rep (22, Sep 14); the correctness/
  counting is later and only on request. Measured by whether the recognition call fires days later, not now.
