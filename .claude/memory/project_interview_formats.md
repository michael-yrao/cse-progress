**Compact recall pointer — the research and the per-company loop table live in
[`docs/foundations/interview_formats.md`](../../docs/foundations/interview_formats.md)** (researched
Sep 26, 2026; re-research when a tier's applications open). Do not restate the landscape here.

- **Two untrained round shapes on the route:** (1) **code comprehension / bug squash / integration**
  (Stripe ×2, Google's Gemini pilot, Datadog, Bloomberg) — every rep in this repo starts from a blank
  file, none starts from someone else's code; (2) **AI-assisted multi-file** (Meta's 60-min round,
  spreading to Google/Microsoft) — scored on directing, verifying and owning the model's output, a
  different skill from working unaided. The unaided phone screen is unchanged everywhere, so the DSA
  engine stays the prerequisite.
- **Practical Coding Rep — PARKED (set 2026-09-26).** One design, two variants (unaided first, AI-assisted
  second), 45–60 min on a coach-supplied small repo with failing tests; first two of each variant
  unrated; evening slot, off the DSA ceiling. **Trigger (gate, not date):** fires at a weekly build when
  BOTH hold — `career_strategy.md` Gate 1's DSA half holds AND the learner says applications are opening
  (their word, like SD mocks). Stripe's Bug Squash is the first practical round on the route, so the
  drill must exist before the fintech loops. **Don't nudge** until then (same discipline as
  [[project_sd_mock_model]] study mode and [[project_ai_pillar]]). Nothing is scaffolded before
  activation — no skill reference, no tracker row type. The firing step sits in the weekly-build
  checklist beside the AI-pillar one.
- **GenAI SD** overlaps the AI pillar's `10-ml-system-design` and the ChatGPT row on the SD board —
  it rides the AI pillar's activation, not a new track. **Behavioural** at L6 is named as a gap and
  out of scope (story-bank work, not spaced reps).
- **Company problem demand** is the sibling artifact: `docs/foundations/dsa/mastery/company_demand.md`,
  regenerated by `scripts/company_demand.py` at the weekly build (network; not a hook). Ties to
  [[project_interview_goal]].
- **Follow-up escalation in the coding round** (Google/Amazon "now change X") is trained by the DSA mock — [[project_dsa_mock]].
